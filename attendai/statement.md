# Problem Statement

## Context
Manual attendance in classrooms is a slow process that consumes valuable lecture time. Moreover, it is susceptible to proxy attendance where students mark their absent peers as present. 

## Objective
Build a robust system that detects and recognizes enrolled students' faces from a live webcam feed and automatically logs their attendance with a timestamp. This system aims to significantly reduce manual effort and eliminate proxy attendance.

## Scope and Features
- **Automated Enrollment**: A streamlined process to capture student details (Name, Roll Number) and their face embedding.
- **Real-Time Recognition**: Processing live video feed to identify students dynamically.
- **Liveness Check**: A basic anti-spoofing mechanism to ensure a static photo cannot be used to fake presence.
- **Attendance Logging and Reporting**: Secure storage of attendance records with functionality to view and export reports.

## Target Users
- **Students**: Quick and frictionless attendance marking upon entering the classroom.
- **Faculty/Administration**: Easy access to accurate attendance logs and analytics.
